# 22 — Differentiators

*What we can claim that most scanners can't. Every line links to its evidence: a test, a ledger row, or a verification archive. **A claim that can't link to evidence stays out.***

**Rules for this file.**
- Each claim is **TRUE**, **PARTIAL** or **PENDING**, and says what would make it TRUE.
- It does not restate numbers that SPEC.md or the ledger own; it links to them.
- When a claim's status changes, the change lands in the same PR as its evidence.
- "Most scanners can't" is a positioning statement, not a measured fact. Only competitor behaviour we have actually observed is cited: the Observatory, SSL Labs and checkdmarc comparisons in the ledger.
